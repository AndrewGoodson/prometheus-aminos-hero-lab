# Prometheus Aminos — brand rules

## Name

**Prometheus Aminos**, in full on first use. Never "Prometheus Peptides" (old name).

## Logo

- **Mark:** the flame on a fluted pillar (`brand/logos/mark-*.svg`). **Wordmark:** PROMETHEUS in a high-contrast serif with a swash R, drawn as paths. **Lockup:** mark + wordmark + the small, letter-spaced AMINOS line.
- White ink on black, black ink on white. In the **home hero only**, the mark may be tinted gold and its flame animated (owner decision, 2026-10-23).
- Never stretch, skew, outline or rotate the logo. Never retype or redraw the wordmark in a font. Keep clear space of about the flame's width on every side.

## Colour

Site (white pages) and hero (black) palettes. All text pairs pass WCAG AA.

## Type

- **Display:** EB Garamond 400/500 (headings, the hero headline). Chosen for its plain, short-tailed Q.
- **UI and body:** Montserrat 400/500/600. Small uppercase labels get 0.18–0.32em letter-spacing.
- Body text at least 16 px; headline 44–112 px depending on layout.

## Look

Exact, classical, unembellished; a laboratory supplier for laboratory buyers. Hairline rules instead of boxes, generous but not wasteful space, no rounded "app" cards, no drop shadows, no pill buttons, nothing that looks like a template shop.

## Voice

Plain, calm, exact. Short sentences. Say what the thing is and the rule that applies, then stop. No exclamation marks, no urgency, no superlatives ("best", "most powerful").

## Approved wording (use as is)

- Stolen from the Gods.
- Research compounds, sold by size. Every batch tested by an independent lab.
- Every batch is tested by an independent third-party lab before it ships.
- Each order includes a QR code that opens that batch's result on the testing lab's own website.
- For research use only. Not for human or veterinary use.
- Not evaluated by the FDA.

## Never write or imply

| Category | Examples of what is forbidden |
|---|---|
| Health and performance | treats, cures, heals, weight or fat loss, muscle growth, anti-aging, performance, strength, energy, recovery, "makes you stronger" |
| Dosing and use | dose, dosage, mg/kg, how to inject, reconstitution, cycles, stacks |
| Safety | safe, non-toxic, no side effects, well tolerated |
| Purity and grade | any purity percentage, "pharmaceutical grade", "99%", "highest purity" |
| Sterility | sterile, endotoxin-free, pyrogen-free |
| Certification | GMP certified, ISO certified, FDA approved, "lab verified" (beyond the approved testing lines above) |
| Disease | any disease or condition name |
| Shipping promises | same-day, guaranteed delivery, cold chain, discreet shipping |

Images follow the same rule: no people, bodies, skin, muscles, syringes, pills, needles or clinical scenes.
