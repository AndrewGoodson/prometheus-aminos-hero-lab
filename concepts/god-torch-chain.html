One note on the chain: I drew it as a peptide chain (amino acids linked one after another, twisting like a helix), not the two-strand DNA ladder. Peptides are amino-acid chains. A DNA ladder would be the wrong molecule, and lab buyers would notice. The god here is a rough outline to show the idea. The final would be a proper gold engraving, drawn by an illustrator in this same style.
